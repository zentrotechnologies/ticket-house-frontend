Refund Policy

Last updated: December 2025

This Refund Policy applies to all ticket purchases made through Ticket House (Zentro Technologies LLP) platform.

1. General Policy

All ticket sales are final unless the event is canceled, rescheduled, or significantly changed as per the event organizer's policy. Refund eligibility is determined by the Event Organizer.

2. Canceled Events

If an event is canceled by the organizer, you may be eligible for a full refund of the ticket price (excluding any convenience/booking fees as specified). Refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

3. Rescheduled Events

If an event is rescheduled, your ticket will typically be valid for the new date. If you are unable to attend, you may request a refund as per the organizer's policy.

4. No-Show Policy

Tickets are non-refundable for no-shows. You are responsible for attending the event at the correct time and venue.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Contact for Refunds

To request a refund, please contact our support team at support@tickethouse.in with your booking details.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
